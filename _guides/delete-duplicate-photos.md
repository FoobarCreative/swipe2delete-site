Duplicate photos creep in from every direction — burst shots, re-saved images, WhatsApp re-downloads, "let me take three just in case." Here's how to clear them out, starting with the tool you already have.

## Option 1: the built-in Duplicates album (iOS 16+)

Open **Photos**, scroll to the **Utilities** section, and tap **Duplicates**.

![Duplicates in the Utilities section](/assets/guides/empty-recently-deleted/recently-deleted.webp)

iOS shows detected duplicates side by side. Tap **Merge** on a pair (or **Select → Merge All**) and iOS keeps the highest-quality version, moving the rest to Recently Deleted.

**What it's good at:** exact copies and very close re-saves.

**What it misses:** near-duplicates — the five almost-identical shots of the same sunset, burst photos, or similar frames taken seconds apart. To iOS those are all "different photos," and for most people they're the real clutter.

## Option 2: a dedicated duplicate finder

For proper similar-photo detection, you need an app that groups photos taken in rapid succession and lets you pick the keeper. Full disclosure — we make one: [Dedupify](https://www.dedupify.app) groups similar shots and duplicates, shows them together, and batch-deletes the ones you don't pick. It's the tool we built before Swipe2delete, for exactly this problem.

## Option 3: the swipe cleanup (gets the rest)

Duplicates are only part of a messy camera roll. The blurry shots, screenshots, and photos-of-receipts aren't duplicates of anything — no duplicate finder will flag them. That's the [month-by-month swipe cleanup](/guides/clean-camera-roll){:target="_self"}: one decision per photo, keep or delete, with a review before anything goes.

## Whichever you use: reclaim the space

Merged and deleted photos land in the Recently Deleted album and keep using storage for up to 30 days. [Empty it](/guides/empty-recently-deleted){:target="_self"} to get the space back immediately.
