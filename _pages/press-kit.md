# Press Kit

## About Swipe2delete

Swipe2delete makes cleaning up your camera roll simple and fun. Swipe right to keep and left to delete. Organize your photos one month at a time. No clutter, just your best moments.

Developed by Foobar Creative, a native iOS app development studio from Adelaide, Australia.

**Developer:** [Foobar Creative](https://foobarcreative.com)<br>
**Platform:** iOS<br>
**Website:** [swipe2delete.com](https://www.swipe2delete.com)<br>
**App Store:** [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6464102619)

## Key Features

- **Swipe to Organize**: Swipe left to delete, right to keep
- **Monthly Cleanup**: Tackle photos one month at a time
- **On This Day Memories**: Relive moments from past years
- **Undo Mistakes**: Tap to undo any accidental deletion
- **Final Review**: Review your choices before confirming
- **Progress Tracker**: See how much you've sorted through
